About TEPIA

12 years of getting people to work safely, legally, on time.

TEPIA GROUP sp. z o.o. is a licensed employment agency headquartered in Warsaw, registered with the Marshal's Office of the Mazovia Voivodeship (KRAZ no. 00000/2014).

Our story

TEPIA started with staffing three warehouses near Warsaw. Today we work with over 120 verified employers in 9 countries and support candidates from Ukraine, Belarus and beyond.

We learned early that people need more than a job listing — they need a guarantee that the contract is real, the housing exists, and the salary shows up on time. Every vacancy is checked in person before it goes on the site.

What Our Trust Is Built On

Three commitments we don't compromise on

Only legal contracts

Verified employers and registered work permits — no shady schemes, ever.

Everything fixed in writing

Salary, housing and schedule are agreed in the contract before you travel.

A manager for the whole contract

Your personal manager stays reachable for as long as your contract runs.
