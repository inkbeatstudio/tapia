TEPIA GROUP sp. z o.o. — Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026. This policy explains how TEPIA GROUP sp. z o.o., with registered office at ul. Złota 44, 00-120 Warszawa, Poland ("TEPIA", "we"), processes personal data of visitors, candidates and clients of tepiagroup.pl.

1. Data we collect

Contact details you submit through our forms (name, phone, email, company), CVs and application materials, and standard technical data (IP address, browser type) collected via cookies once you consent.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to consultation and mandate requests
  • To match candidates with verified, open vacancies abroad
  • To send newsletter updates to subscribers who opted in

3. Legal basis

Processing is based on your consent, on steps necessary prior to entering a contract, and on our legitimate interest in operating an employment agency business.

4. Retention

Candidate data is retained for up to 24 months unless you request earlier deletion. Lead and contact-form data is retained for up to 12 months.

5. Your rights

Under GDPR you may request access, correction, deletion or portability of your data by writing to privacy@tepiagroup.pl.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to privacy@tepiagroup.pl or by post to our Warsaw office.
